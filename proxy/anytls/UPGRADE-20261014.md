`outbound_external_controls_test.go` and the new
`outbound_external_readiness_test.go`. AnyTLS runtime and shared Hy2 code were
not changed. The former helper returned after TCP connect succeeded. Mihomo
binds its listener before completing provider loading and entering tunnel
Running; during that interval it accepts connections but closes business
streams. The cached binary supports `-post-up`, executed after configuration

post-up receipt before checking the listener. It does not send a warm-up
request, retry an acceptance exchange, extend a business deadline, or hide
